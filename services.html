Craftsmanship Workflow

The Sartoria Bespoke Journey

From the initial private measurement salon to the final steam hand-press, every garment undergoes an uncompromising four-step artisanal cycle.

Stage 01

Anatomical Measurement

Over 30 distinct body measurements recorded along with posture profiling, shoulder balance, and lifestyle preference.

Book Fitting
Stage 02

Fabric & Pattern Drafting

Curate your textiles from our 500+ heritage swatch library before our master cutter drafts your individual paper pattern.

Browse Fabrics
Stage 03

Basted Trial Fitting

A mid-construction trial garment assembled with white baste thread to sculpt collar pitch, waist suppression, and drape.

Fitting Details
Stage 04

Hand Finishing & Delivery

Silk buttonholes hand-stitched, pick-stitched lapels, hand-pressed with industrial gravity iron, and encased in luxury garment bag.
