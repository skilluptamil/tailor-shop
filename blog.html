Atelier Care Guide

Garment Care & Fabric Longevity

Preserve the structure, drape, and tactile richness of your bespoke wardrobe with our master artisan preservation standards.

Pure Wool & Canvas

Never machine wash or heavily dry clean. Air out on contoured cedar hangers and gently steam to refresh fibers.

Mulberry Silk & Linings

Store away from UV sunlight in 100% breathable cotton bags. Protect metallic threads from direct moisture and perfume.

Linen & Fine Cottons

Embrace the relaxed natural crease of Irish linen. Light steam pressing while slightly damp restores sharp collars and cuffs.

Seasonal Seam Check

Bring bespoke pieces in every 6 months for complimentary seam tightening, button reinforcement, and deep steam shaping.
