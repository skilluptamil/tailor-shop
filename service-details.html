Men's Bespoke 2 & 3-Piece Suits

Uncompromising Savile Row Quality Tailoring

Our bespoke suiting service is the ultimate synthesis of traditional British tailoring architecture and Italian softness. Hand-cut from individual 2D paper patterns drafted solely for your silhouette, each suit features a floating horsehair canvas chest piece that naturally molds to your body shape over time.

Whether you are commissioning a boardroom navy wool suit, an opulent velvet smoking jacket, or a black-tie peak lapel tuxedo, our master tailors guide you through every choice of fabric weight, drape, shoulder construction, and horn button selection.

Master Craftsmanship Features

Full Floating Canvas

Natural horsehair canvas stitched inside the chest for superior drape and breathable longevity.

Milanese Buttonhole

Each lapel buttonhole is meticulously hand-sewn with silk gimp thread for a raised luxury finish.

2 Intermediate Fittings

Includes a basted fitting and forward fitting to ensure sleeve pitch and waist drape are immaculate.

Natural Horn Buttons

Ethically sourced genuine horn or mother-of-pearl buttons with cross-stitch anchoring.

What's Included in the Bespoke Package

  • Individual 30-Point Anatomical Pattern Drafting
    Your unique paper pattern is archived permanently in our studio for future orders.
  • Choice of 200+ Super 130s-150s Merino Wool Fabrics

  • Bespoke Cupro or Pure Silk Jacquard Lining
    Contrast colors, botanical motifs, or classic tonal weaves.
  • Complimentary Monogramming & Garment Bag
    Hand-embroidered initials inside jacket breast pocket + cedar hanger.
